# Landing map — rebuild harness

Generated by `tools/landing_map.py`. Do not edit by hand.

`docs/label-schedule.md` gives both ends of every wire as `RR-1` and `PDM-5`. This resolves those to the **physical part and the hole in it**: what the thing is, what its MPN is, which cavity, and where it sits on the bike.

Positions and cavity names come from `tools/cut_list.py`, the part identity and pin labels from the model, so this cannot disagree with the cut list, the label schedule or the BOM.

⚠️ **A splice's physical form is not repeated here.** What each `SP_` node actually is — a parallel crimp, a sealed junction, the stock bullet at B03 — and when it can be crimped is the **splices section of `docs/cut-list.md`**, which is the authority.
