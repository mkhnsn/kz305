# Branch map — rebuild harness

Generated by `tools/branch_map.py`. Do not edit by hand.

The WireViz drawings are **schematics** — they say what connects to what and deliberately say nothing about physical routing. This is the other view, and it is the one the build needs: **which wires travel together**, so a branch can be cut, sleeved and taped as a unit.

Positions are `tools/cut_list.py`'s `POSITIONS`, so this cannot drift from the cut list. A wire is placed on the trunk by its two ends and occupies everything between them; a wire with both ends at one breakout never enters the trunk and is counted only in that breakout's drop.

⚠️ **Conductor counts are exact; diameters are not.** The count is from the model. The OD is estimated as `D = d × √(N / 0.75)` from the recorded insulation ODs — 16 AWG TXL calipered **2.20–2.21 mm**, 14 AWG TXL **2.59–2.70** (worst case taken), `W_ALT` **2.36** as the one ETFE run. Measure a real bundle before trusting a sleeve at its limit.
