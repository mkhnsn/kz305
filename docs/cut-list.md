# Cut list — rebuild harness

Generated by `tools/cut_list.py`. Do not edit by hand.

**PDM breakout: 835 mm from the headlight datum**, the old B06 fan-out beside the battery. Decided 15 Sep 2026. The PDM, the battery and the regulator all sit in a custom box where the old battery box was. Decided 17 Sep 2026.

Every length is **route + margin, rounded up to 50 mm**, and the route is the 28 Aug branch map: trunk distance between the two ends plus each end's branch off the trunk. The PDM or relay end is crimped at the bench and does not move; **the far end is trimmed on the bike**, which is what the 500 mm margin is for - loose, because the build starts on the bench with nothing mounted. A wire with both ends at the PDM (route 0) is a bench jumper and is cut to 300 mm.

**Basis:** `tape` — both ends were measured on the old harness. `est` — at least one end is a part the old harness did not have, or one whose lead was not taped, placed by where it sits on the bike. The `est` rows carry the same margin; if a part ends up far from where the table assumes, fix `POSITIONS` in the tool and re-run before cutting that wire.

⚠️ **Cut one wire, label it, seal it, crimp the PDM end, then cut the next.** A cut wire with no label is the one that gets mixed up.
